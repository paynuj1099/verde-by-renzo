Few golf accessories are as recognizable or as versatile as the cap.

It provides practical coverage while also becoming an important part of a golfer's personal style.

The strongest designs do both without demanding attention.

## A Versatile Silhouette

A clean, structured cap works across a wide range of golf outfits.

It can complement performance apparel on the course and remain appropriate with casual clothing afterward.

The simplicity of the silhouette is what gives it longevity.

## Color Changes the Character

Color can significantly change the personality of an outfit.

- **Forest Green** creates a signature VERDE look.
- **Navy Blue** feels timeless and classic.
- **Cream** provides a softer finish.
- **Khaki** works naturally with neutral outfits.
- **Black** creates a minimal appearance.
- **White** keeps the look clean and traditional.
- **Burgundy** introduces a deeper statement color.

Changing only the cap can significantly alter the overall character of an outfit.

## Branding With Restraint

Embroidered branding works best when it feels integrated into the product rather than placed on top of it.

The VERDE approach uses the logo as a finishing detail instead of allowing it to overpower the design.
