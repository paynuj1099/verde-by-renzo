Performance apparel is designed to work hard during a round, which means it also deserves proper care afterward.

A few simple habits can help preserve the appearance and comfort of your golf clothing.

## Wash With Similar Colors

Separating light and dark garments remains one of the simplest ways to protect their appearance.

For darker golf apparel, washing with similar colors can help minimize unwanted color transfer.

## Choose a Gentle Cycle

Cold water and a gentle wash cycle are generally suitable for performance garments when consistent with the garment's care label.

Aggressive washing is rarely necessary for everyday golf wear.

## Avoid Excessive Heat

Whenever possible, allow performance garments to air dry.

Excessive heat can be unnecessarily harsh on technical fabrics over time.

## Keep It Simple

For the VERDE Performance Polo:

- Machine wash cold
- Wash with similar colors
- Do not bleach
- Air dry when possible

Always follow the specific care instructions supplied with the garment.
